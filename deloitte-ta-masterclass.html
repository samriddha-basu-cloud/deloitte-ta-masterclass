Module 2 · End-to-End Talent Acquisition Syllabus

Tap each topic to expand.

2.1 Strategic Workforce Planning & Requisition Management
Demand forecasting: convert project pipeline (RFP wins, bench strength, attrition projections) into requisitions.

Buy vs Build vs Borrow

  • Buy: lateral hiring for immediate deployment
  • Build: campus hiring (Tier-1/2 B-schools, engineering colleges) and internal upskilling
  • Borrow: contingent workforce, contractors, internal mobility / bench deployment

Job intake strategy (30-minute call)

  • Must-have vs good-to-have skills
  • Target competitor firms (Big 4, Tier-1 IT services, boutiques)
  • Deal-breakers, billing/project start timelines, panel availability
  • Compensation range, location/work mode, headcount phasing
Add-on: Close every intake by agreeing on an SLA — profiles within X days, feedback within 48 hours — and document it in an intake sheet.
2.2 Sourcing Strategies & Talent Intelligence
Active vs passive: job boards (Naukri, LinkedIn Recruiter, IIMJobs) for active candidates; talent mapping, GitHub, Kaggle, alumni networks for passive talent.

Channel effectiveness: Employee referrals (often highest conversion and retention), career portal, RPO vendors, niche hackathons.
2.3 Talent Assessment & Competency-Based Interviewing
  • BEI: past behaviour is the best predictor of future performance.
  • STAR / SOAR: Situation/Obstacle → Task/Action → Result. Separate "I" from "We"; quantify results.
  • Psychometrics & skill tests: coding tests, case studies, personality inventories (Hogan, OPQ, Saville) for cognitive agility and leadership potential.
Add-on: Structured interviews with a common scorecard and rating anchors beat unstructured ones for validity and bias reduction.
2.4 Offer Management, Compensation & Post-Offer Engagement
  • Structure: fixed vs variable (performance-linked incentive), joining bonus (with clawback), relocation, notice-period buyout.
  • Compa-ratio = (Proposed fixed salary ÷ Pay-band midpoint) × 100. 80–120% is standard; above 100% needs justification (niche skill, competing offers, immediate joiner) to protect internal equity.
  • Drop-off mitigation: with 60–90 day notice periods, ghosting and offer-shopping are major risks. Use bi-weekly touchpoints, a buddy/HM connect, newsletters, and watch red flags such as reluctance to share a resignation acceptance.
2.5 Campus Recruitment (University Relations)
  • Employer branding: case competitions (e.g. Deloitte Maverick), Partner guest lectures, live projects, social campaigns.
  • Day-Zero / Slot-Zero: compete at premier campuses; use PPOs from summer internships to reduce final-placement pressure.
Add-on lifecycle: campus mapping → pre-placement talk → test/case round → GD/PI → offers → pre-joining engagement → onboarding → first-year tracking.
2.6 HR Technology & AI in TA
  • ATS/CRM: Workday Recruiting, SAP SuccessFactors, Avature, Eightfold.ai, Phenom.
  • GenAI in TA: JD calibration, resume parsing and skill matching, interview scheduling, predictive attrition modelling.
  • Ethical AI & bias: make sure tools don't penalise career breaks (e.g. maternity returnees) or non-traditional education. Keep humans in the loop, audit models regularly, and keep decisions explainable.
2.7 Add-on: Compliance, BGV & Labour Codes (India)
  • Four Labour Codes: Wages, Industrial Relations, Social Security, Occupational Safety, Health & Working Conditions (OSHWC). Be aware of fixed-term employment and gig/platform worker definitions.
  • BGV: education, employment, address, criminal/court checks; conflicts and discrepancies must be escalated and handled consistently.
  • Data privacy: candidate data handled under applicable data-protection law (India's DPDP Act) and firm policy.
Keep this high-level; confirm current rollout status of the Labour Codes before citing specifics.

Module 6 · Questions to Ask & Pre-Interview Checklist

Never say "I have no questions." Pick two based on who is interviewing you.

TA Manager / Senior Recruiter
  1. How is the TA team integrating AI or automation into sourcing and screening, and how has that changed the recruiter's day-to-day role?
  2. Which KPIs separate a top-performing TA professional from an average one in the first six months?
  3. What does a typical first 90 days look like for a new TA joiner?
TA Director / Partner
  1. With demand shifting to AI and cloud, how does TA balance building talent through campuses versus buying laterally?
  2. What is the biggest talent challenge your service line expects over the next two quarters?
  3. How does TA partner with the business on workforce planning?

Module 7 · Frameworks & Quick Revision

Scenario answers

Clarify (ask 2–3 questions) → Diagnose (root cause with data) → Action plan (immediate + short-term) → Prevent (process fix).

STAR+

Situation, Task, Action (your "I"), Result (numbers), plus Learning (what you'd do differently).

Iron Triangle

Speed, Cost, Quality: you can rarely maximise all three. Offer trade-off options with data.

Talent advisor mindset

Challenge with market data, propose options, own outcomes, don't just take orders.

Candidate experience

Fast, transparent, respectful: timely feedback, clear process, honest role preview.

Answer structure

Headline first, then 2–3 points, then an example. Aim for 90 seconds to 2 minutes.

Flashcards (tap to flip)

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Saying "I'd just tell the manager no" instead of proposing data-backed options.
  • Using "we" throughout so your own contribution is unclear.
  • Quoting metrics you can't explain or defend.
  • Generic "why Deloitte" answers with no knowledge of service lines or entity.
  • Ignoring candidate experience, compliance and DEI in scenario answers.
